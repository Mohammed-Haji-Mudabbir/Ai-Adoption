## Project Overview

Artificial Intelligence is transforming modern workplaces. But how effectively are organizations adopting AI tools, Microsoft Copilot, and AI agents?

**AI Adoption & Business Intelligence Analytics** explores AI adoption, user engagement, usage patterns, and agent performance through an interactive Power BI reporting solution.

This portfolio project is based on Microsoft's open-source AI-in-One Dashboard, with its original resources and licensing preserved.

The objective is to demonstrate how data analytics and business intelligence can transform complex datasets into meaningful insights that support better business decisions.
